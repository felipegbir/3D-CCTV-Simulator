- Maximum open camera viewports increased from 5 to 16.
- Selecting a camera raises its open viewport.
- Clicking or interacting with a viewport raises it.
- Maximizing or minimizing a viewport raises it first, preventing older creation order from covering it.
- Operations > View includes `▦ Arrange Camera Wall`.
- Wall capacity follows 1x1 for one viewport, 2x2 for 2-4, 3x3 for 5-9, and 4x4 for 10-16.
- Wall arrangement resets minimized/maximized state safely and recalculates on browser resize while the wall remains active.
- Dragging, minimizing, or maximizing exits wall-layout mode so manual placement remains available.
- Minimized viewports skip rendering and closed viewport renderers are disposed.
