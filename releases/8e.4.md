## Preference contract

- Preferences UI is available under Operations > Preferences.
- Supported settings: theme, Reverse Pan, Reverse Tilt, Invert Zoom, renderer quality, grid visibility, axes visibility, projection-cone opacity, and FBX auto-scale.
- Settings apply without code changes and persist in browser local storage under `nomadCctvPreferences.v1`.
- Stored values are type-checked, enums are allow-listed, and cone opacity is clamped to 5-50%.
- Reset Preferences restores the accepted defaults.
- High quality preserves the prior pixel-ratio/shadow behavior; Balanced caps pixel ratio at 1.5; Performance caps it at 1 and disables main-renderer shadows.

## Persistence boundary

- Project schema remains version 2 and is not silently expanded.
- Browser preferences are independent of project JSON in this release.
- The owner-requested final persistence reconciliation remains deferred: camera viewport palettes/modes and external model/reference-image path-plus-transform restoration will be handled after the numbered roadmap work.
